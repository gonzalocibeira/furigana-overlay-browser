A Japanese reading companion

About Kō

A little help, wherever you read.

一About the project

Kō helps you read Japanese on webpages and in Google Docs. It adds furigana above kanji, offers a roomier reading panel, and remembers your preferred pronunciations while keeping the original text and formatting intact.

Built by Gonzalo Cibeira to make reading Japanese in Google Docs easier, then extended to help wherever you browse. The project is open source under the MIT license.

二Reading settings

Open Kō from Chrome’s extensions toolbar to pause automatic readings, choose your reading appearance, enable the reading panel, or manage local pronunciation corrections. Site settings apply to the webpage you have open.

三Privacy

Furigana runs on your device with bundled dictionaries. Settings and corrections are stored locally. Opening a word definition sends that word’s dictionary form to English and Japanese Wiktionary, which also receives your IP address. Surrounding sentences and full documents are not uploaded. Kō has no analytics, advertising, or extension account.
